Major focus of KLB's international banking

As the Bank considers international banking as one of its main strategic areas, KLB's International Banking Department has recently focused on the following strategic business areas to increase its efficiency in the field of international banking business.

Offshore Syndicated Loan : KLB assists domestic clients to finance business projects abroad by providing offshore loans for their overseas subsidiaries and joint-ventures. KLB also acts as an arranger or co-arranger of syndicated loans for foreign corporations and banks. The Bank's most notable achievement in this field is that a growing number of foreign clients are utilizing KLB's syndicated loans. Overall, KLB provides a stable source of financing in international capital markets at competitive rates.

Capital Market Origination : KLB furnishes wide-ranging support for overseas issuances in international capital markets. Clients benefit from the Bank's expertise in all aspects of issuance from preparatory work to administrative procedures. To enlarge the scope of the Bank's international activities, KLB establishes offshore investment funds and strengthens its intermediary role in structured financing.

Project Finance : With its history of infrastructure development financing in Korea, KLB is well suited to provide its clients with a broad range of advisory and financing services for individual projects. The Bank covers a wide range of projects from manufacturing plants to social infrastructure carried out by foreign as well as Korean companies.

Dealing : As an institutional investor, KLB manages a portfolio of investment-grade securities traded in overseas financial markets. Backed by the highest level of financial analysis, KLB's international securities portfolio has gained recognition for producing strong financial results each year.

Derivatives : To allow its clients to pursue sound financing strategies, KLB provides derivative instruments to its corporate clients to assist them with the management of interest and foreign exchange risk within acceptable levels. The Bank offers a comprehensive selection of derivative products including options, futures and swaps.

Custody Services : As the ceiling on foreign investment in the Korean stock market is raised, the demand for securities custody services from foreign institutional and individual investors will grow rapidly. KLB is one of a handful of Korean banks authorized to hold Korean stocks on behalf of foreign investors through its custodial and proxy services.
