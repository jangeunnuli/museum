Q:What kind of insurance coverage does KLB offer?

A: Securities are insured with a full coverage of KRW 30 Mil(USD Equi 34 Mil) against securities loss. Custody staffs carry the insurance coverage of KRW 50 Mil(USD Equi 57,000) against embezzlement, dereliction of duty,and extra.



Q:How to change a sub-custodian bank?

A:Once you give the instruction for change of sub-custodian bank to the old sub-custodian bank and new sub-custodian bank, the two concerned banks will report this change of sub-custodianship to BOK and KSSB. New 'power of attorney' & 'sub-custodian agreement' should be signed by you and new custodian bank. For the safe transfer of securities and cash, KSD system and wire money transfer will be used. I t usually takes 2 to 3 days to finish all these activities to change sub-custodianship.
