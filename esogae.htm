Brief of KLB


1. Brief History of KLB


Apr. 1967 Incorporated as Korea Development Finance Corporation
Feb. 1968 Registered as a foreign-invested enterprise with equity participation by the IFC and other foreign banks
May. 1969 Listed its shares on the Korea Stock Exchange
Jun. 1971 Established Korea Investment & Finance Corporation (presently, Hana Bank)
Jun. 1980 Transformed into Korea Long Term Credit Bank
Nov. 1987 Established KLB Credit Card Co.,Ltd.
Oct. 1988 Obtained trust business license
Mar 1989 Obtained trustee licenses for general bond trust and securities investment trust
Oct. 1989 Established KLB Asia Finance Ltd.and KLB Investment Management Co.,Ltd.
Apr. 1990 Established KLB Investment Co.,Ltd
Sep. 1991 Established KLB Economic Research Institute
Nov. 1991 Established Korea Long Term Credit Bank International Ltd.
Dec. 1991 Amendment and enforcement of the "Long Term Credit Bank Law"
Dec. 1994 Established KLB Rental Co.,Ltd.
Oct. 1995 Established KLB Installment Finance Co.,Ltd.
Sep. 1996 KLB Investment management Co. Ltd. was transformed into KLB Investment Trust Management Co. Ltd.
May 1997 KLB Futures Co. Ltd. was established
July 1997 KLB Public Foundation was established
