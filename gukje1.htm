Summary of KLB's international banking business


As continuing integration of international financial markets followed by globalization and securitization of international banking opens new avenues for growth and brings sweeping changes to the global arena, domestic and foreign clients are seeking sophisticated low cost financial services suited to their needs. Seeing opportunity for future growth in these changing environments, KLB is redoubling efforts to diversify its products and to secure advanced financial techniques. KLB is also creating services to meet the overseas financial needs of corporate clients. As a key strategic area, KLB's expansion of international banking activities is an important source of future growth.
