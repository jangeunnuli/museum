Investment Environment


  • How to invest in Korea.
Direct foreign investment is regulated by the Foreign Currency Inducement Act,while portfolio investment follows guidelines set by the Regulation on Trading of Securities by Foreigners(RTSF) and the Foreign Exchange Management Regulation(FEMR). In order for foreign investors to invest in Korea,they must appoint one local bank or securities co.as a standing agent and one bank for foreign exchange bank. Usually, fx bank acts as a standing agent. KLB as the most stable standing agent and custodian bank, we arrange registration for Investment Registration Card(IRC) issued by Korea Securities Supervisory Board(KSSB) and an exemption from the Entrustment Guarantee Deposit(EGD) requirement with Korea Stock Exchange if the investor is institutional.The Foreign Investors are also required to open KRW & USD A/C with local foreign exchange bank and a standing agent may open trade A/C with a broker co.
