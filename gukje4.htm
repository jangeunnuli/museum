    OVERSEAS NETWORK

    In order to fully support overseas corporate clients, KLB is further enhancing its international competitiveness by reinforcing current overseas operations and establishing new network abroad. As of the end of September, 1997, KLB had overseas representative offices in HongKong and Qingdao, Ho Chi Minh, Yangon branch offices in Tokyo, New York, Singapore and the Cayman Islands, and merchant banking subsidiaries in Hong Kong and London.

    KLB plans to upgrade its representative office in Qingdao in 1998. With these expansions, KLB will have secured operation bases in key markets around the world and an overseas network for 24-hour trading.

    Each overseas network focuses on performing its specialized field as follows ;

    1) KLB Asia Finance Ltd. & KLB, Singapore Branch

    : Syndicated Loan & Project Financing

    2) KLB International Ltd. & KLB, New York Branch

    : International Trading Center

    3) KLB, Tokyo Branch & KLB, Qingdao Representative Office and Others

    : Local Business Center

    4) KLB, Cayman Branch

    : Off-shore Financial Transaction

    Until now, we have concentrated on the establishment of our overseas networks in financial centers around world. In additon, we are now trying to put our focus on emerging market like Indonesia, China, Vietnam by the method of M&A, Joint Venture and Branch operation.
