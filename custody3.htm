Overview of KLB Custody


As your custodian, we offer the following custody services in order to become your valuable partner.

- Registering with the Securities Supervisory Board(SSB) of Korea and obtaining an Investment Registration Card(IRC) with a requisite investor's identification number.

- Safekeeping ; KLB assumes full responsibility for the safety of securities in custody.

- Registering on your behalf with appropriate bodies, collecting interest, dividends, redemption payments and crediting to your A/C.

- Effecting settlement of purchases and sales, and giving confirmation of each transaction.

- Report of cash administration on each time cash balances change (Korean Won A/C and Foreign Currency A/C with KLB).

- Foreign exchange dealing in conjunction with settlement.

- Valuation of securities being kept by KLB.

- Informing you twice beforehand of corporate actions affecting securities.

- Applying for tax exemptions, where applicable.

- Executing your standing instructions regarding specific transactions

- Providing you with information on regulatory changes on time.

- Insurance : KRW30,000 million(equivalent of USD34 mil) against each occurrence of securities loss.

- Repatriation immediately.
