Fast Growth


Along with the opening of the Korean stock market to foreign investors in 1992, KLB has extended its focus on serving global customers for their maximum benefits in securities investment in Korea. Such efforts, coupled with the outstanding reputation for tailored services, have made KLB the second largest custody bank in Korea. Now, KLB is the second largest custodian bank and continues to follow the path of strong and steady growth.






Firm Reputation


Korea Long Term Credit Bank (KLB) is highly reputed among financial institutions for its efficient operations and sound management. Its stock price has always stood on top in banking industry , a reflection of excellent performance records resulting from its thorough understanding of the industry and the economy.
